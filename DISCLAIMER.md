# Disclaimer

This is one person's read of one firmware build, not a promise. The write-up, the how-to and the scripts in `tools/` describe what was found on a Samsung Galaxy Tab A8 (SM-X200) with the stock builds named in the [teardown](Tab-A8-teardown.md). Another build, region or model can differ.

## Flashing is at your own risk

Unlocking the bootloader wipes the tablet, and flashing can leave it unable to boot. Samsung may refuse warranty work on a tablet that was unlocked. Read the whole [how-to](docs/lineageos-howto.md) before you start, keep a way back, and check each step against your own device.
